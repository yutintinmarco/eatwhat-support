食咩好 EatWhat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

食咩好 EatWhat is designed to work primarily with data stored locally on your device.

Data we collect

We do not create user accounts and we do not operate an analytics, advertising, or tracking system in version 1.0.

Data stored on your device

The app may store choices, restaurant records, custom pools, selection history, settings, and user-selected photos locally on your device. This information is used only to provide app functionality.

Photos

If you choose to add a photo to a food item, restaurant, or pool, the selected image is processed and stored locally by the app. The app does not upload your selected photos to our own servers.

Third-party advertising and analytics

Version 1.0 does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete or modify it in the app, or remove the app and its local data from your device, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior.

Children

The app is a general food and restaurant decision utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact: eatwhat.hk.support@gmail.com
